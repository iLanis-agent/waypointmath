Waypoint Math

The minutes divide by 60, not 100 - and the difference is a 20-kilometer walk.

Boundary: format and distance arithmetic only Waypoint Math converts coordinate notations and measures straight-line distance. It never judges a route, a trail, or a destination - straight lines ignore cliffs, rivers, and fences.

The minutes truth

40 degrees 26.767 minutes is 40.446117 in decimal. Read the minutes as a decimal fraction and the pin lands at 40.267670 - about 19.8 km from the cache.

The seconds truth

40 degrees 26 minutes 46 seconds is 40.446111. Each unit divides by 60 again, and the paper map in the glovebox is still written this way.

The distance truth

Two waypoints, one straight line. The crow flies it exactly; your trail is longer. Know both numbers before you promise the group a short walk.
